Failed rerouting retains accepted instruction progress internally while withholding guidance; recovery to the previous route does not restart the turn sequence. Duplicate ticks do not count; inconsistent/reversed timestamps, jumps above 55 m/s plus 10 m allowance, signal gaps and recovery into the uncertainty corridor reset evidence. The whole driving geometry is considered conservatively at intersections/nearby legs; planned green connections and preview selection are never used.
- Sustained deviation reuses the existing Directions controller/token to request from the latest eligible GPS to the current incomplete stop. Replacement attempts have a 30-second cooldown, one in-flight request, the existing 15-second timeout and general five-second request guard. Confident recovery onto that reference (`distance + accuracy ≤40 m`, with a distinct fix within six seconds and plausible motion) cancels pending rerouting and resumes the previous route, without claiming a replacement succeeded. Ineligible GPS, Pause/Finish/completion, blur/background and destination/session changes cancel work and clear output/evidence. No extra GPS watcher, persistence or backend call was added.
- Limitations: local polyline distance is not road-level map matching. Close parallel roads and intersections deliberately delay detection; sustained large GPS bias can still produce a false deviation, and movement within the full route corridor (including backward travel) is not detected as wrong-way driving. Recovery requires a distinct eligible fix with plausible motion from the preceding fix within six seconds.
